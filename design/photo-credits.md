# Card photos and their credits

Openly licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons (Abram, 30 Sep 2026: "use openly licensed photos for the cards, with credits; I'll swap in my own later"). Each photo was chosen by Claude, checked against the card's place by the file's title and description, and its licence read from Commons' own metadata on 30 Sep 2026. Only CC0, public domain, CC BY and CC BY-SA are used (no NC or ND). Changes: resized to 1200 px wide; nothing else. The app shows the credit under each card's picture, linked to the source page. **To swap in your own photo:** replace `app/Sources/Photos/<card>.jpg` and its entry in `photo-credits.json`.
