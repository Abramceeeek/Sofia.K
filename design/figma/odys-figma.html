ODYS · the whole app, for review

Every screen rebuilt as editable layers, in the night light and the day light. The colours follow London's real light on the phone (D130), so each screen exists in both. Built 25 Oct 2026 from the app's own words, colours and example day. Card photos are drawn as fields of colour; the real screens, photos included, are on the screenshot board.

What's here
Foundations, the five skies, first run and onboarding, the plan, home before / on / after the day, “Now” on the way and arrived, rating, the end of the day, a trip, You, Help & safety, notifications, permissions, errors and waiting.
Frames
402 × 874 points (iPhone 16 Pro). Long screens run to their full height. Android uses the same layout with the system's own bars.
Fonts
Newsreader (Google Fonts) and Inter. On the phone the system font is SF Pro (iPhone) or Roboto (Android).
Comment freely
Change anything. If a change from this file goes into the app, Abram needs a short signed note from you first (the contributor deed, Part A).

Motion (not in Figma, so written down)
Settle · 450 ms
Content rises 10 pt as it fades in; lists stagger by 60 ms.
The arrival · 0.26 s, then 1.1 s
Tapping “I'm here” (or the phone noticing): the way in gives way to what to notice, and a warm light opens out from the top once, like coming out of the station into the open.
Breathe · 6 s loop
The glow behind the arch on “Now” slowly brightens and dims.
Rating · 0.7 s
The arch fills with light up to the chosen word; on Save one small light rises into it (600 ms).
Words · 70 ms a word
Reactions come into focus from a blur, in amber italic.
Lift · 700 ms
The share card tilts up into place.
Reduce Motion
Every movement becomes a plain fade; nothing moves on its own.
Haptics
One light tap on main actions and on arriving; nothing else.
