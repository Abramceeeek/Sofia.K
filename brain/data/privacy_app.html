Privacy notice: Days out (test app)

Last updated: 29 September 2026. This covers the Days out test app, which is in testing through Apple's TestFlight.

Who is responsible

Abram, an individual in London, is testing an idea for a travel planning app. This is research: nothing is sold. Contact: abram@abdurakhmonbek.com.

What we collect

  • Your answers in the app: the best hour of a recent day out, why you go out, your reactions to 16 London places, your 10 "this or that" choices, and the optional "about you" and photo questions.
  • The day plans the app makes for you, and the 1–5 ratings you give each stop.
  • A few app events, such as finishing the questions or asking for another plan, with their time.
  • Anything you write in Help & safety.

We don't ask for your name, email or phone number. The app knows your phone only by a random code, kept in the phone's secure storage. We don't ask for your nationality, health details or allergies. Please don't enter them.

Why, and on what basis

To plan days out that fit you, and to test whether this works. The lawful basis is your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your data in the app.

Who else handles it

  • Render hosts our server and database in Frankfurt, in the EU.
  • Apple delivers the app through TestFlight, under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.
  • No AI service receives your answers in this version of the app.

How long we keep it

Until 31 March 2027, then it's deleted. Anonymised results may be kept. Settings → Delete my data in the app removes everything we hold about your phone, straight away.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, or object, by using the app or by emailing abram@abdurakhmonbek.com. We reply within one month. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Safety

In an emergency, call 999. For anything else that feels unsafe on a day out, use Help & safety in the app: a person replies within a few hours.
