A small independent London project

Which London places did you actually enjoy?

30 places, about 2 minutes. For each one, say how it felt: loved it, it was fine, didn't enjoy it, or haven't been.

Why: I'm building a day planner that learns what makes a day out feel good for each person. Your answers help pick the places it asks new people about.

  • No login, no sign-up
  • No questions about you
  • We don't store your IP address

What we store, and for how long

Your browser makes a random ID and keeps it on this device, so your answers stay together and you can delete them. It isn't linked to your name, device or account.

We store that ID, your answer for each place, and the date (not the time). We don't store your IP address, and we don't use cookies, analytics or trackers.

The site runs on Render, whose servers handle each visit and may keep their own technical logs. We don't use them.

The answers are used only to choose places for the app. We delete them by 31 March 2027 at the latest; counts per place may be kept.

Delete your answers any time with the button at the end. Questions: the person who shared this link with you.
