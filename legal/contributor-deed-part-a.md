**This deed is made by:**
- Abram [surname], London ("the Founder"), for himself and for the company he intends to incorporate to run the project ("the Company");
- [contributor's full name], of [address] ("the Contributor").

**The project:** a day planner that predicts how places and moments will make each person feel, and plans around it. It includes the app, backend, designs, content, data and brand ("the Project").

1. **Voluntary and flexible.**
   - The Contributor decides whether, when and how much to contribute.
   - There are no set hours and no duty to do any work, and either side may stop at any time.
   - There is no pay. Expenses are repaid only if the Founder agrees to them in writing beforehand.
   - This deed does not create employment.

2. **IP belongs to the Project.**
   - The Contributor assigns to the Founder, with full title guarantee, all copyright, design rights, database rights and other intellectual property in everything they create for the Project (the "Work").
   - This covers Work done before the date of this deed, and future Work as it is created (CDPA 1988 ss.90–91).
   - The Founder holds the Work for the Company, and will assign it to the Company once it is incorporated. The Contributor will sign any document needed to confirm this.
   - The Contributor will use third-party material in the Work only if they have the right to, and will tell the Founder about any such material or licence.

3. **Moral rights.** The Contributor waives their moral rights in the Work (CDPA 1988 s.87), for the Founder, the Company, and their licensees and successors. The Founder will credit the Contributor where it is practical.

4. **Confidentiality.**
   - The Contributor keeps the Project's non-public information confidential, during their involvement and afterwards. This includes plans, code, data, and anything about users or participants.
   - They use it only for the Project, and return or delete it when they stop.

5. **Personal data** (this clause matters most for the safety backup helper).
   - The Contributor sees personal data about users or participants only as needed, and only on the Founder's instructions.
   - They keep it on a device locked with a passcode, and never copy it elsewhere.
   - They never use it to contact anyone for another reason.
   - They tell the Founder within 24 hours of any loss or wrong disclosure, and delete the data when they stop.
   - The helper follows the safety procedure (D46): tell the person to call 999 in an emergency, and pass every alert to the Founder.

6. **No authority.** The Contributor can't sign contracts, spend money or make promises for the Founder or the Company.

7. **Nothing else is promised.** Equity or any other reward is dealt with only in separate written terms (Part B), which are not yet agreed. **This deed doesn't promise equity or payment, and doesn't depend on them.**

8. **Stopping.**
   - Either side may end the Contributor's involvement at any time, in writing; an email is enough.
   - Clauses 2–5 continue after that.
   - Work created before then stays assigned.

9. **Law.** The law of England and Wales governs this deed, and the courts of England and Wales decide any dispute about it.

### Notes for Abram (not part of the deed)

- **Why a deed:**
  - Under this part the contributor gets nothing back: no pay, and no equity yet. In English law, a promise with nothing given in return is generally enforceable only if it is made by deed. *That is Claude's understanding, UNVERIFIED; check it with the solicitor.*
  - A deed also helps the future-copyright clause. s.91 vests future copyright where the assignee "would be entitled as against all other persons to require the copyright to be vested in him".
  - A cheaper alternative, also to check, is a nominal payment of £1.
- **Formalities (checked on legislation.gov.uk, 2026-09-26):**
  - An assignment of copyright must be in writing and signed by the assignor (CDPA s.90(3)).
  - An individual's deed must say on its face that it is a deed. It must be signed in the presence of a witness who attests the signature, and delivered as a deed. No seal is needed (Law of Property (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1989 s.1).
- **The hours clause was removed at your request.** Contributions are voluntary and flexible. That should also lower the risk of a contributor counting as a "worker" for minimum-wage purposes. *Judgement; still one of the solicitor questions.*
- **One copy per person:** the designer and the backup helper each sign their own.
