# Contributor Agreement (DRAFT v0.1, 2026-09-26)

> **Draft for discussion, not legal advice.** Claude wrote it from the brief (D70). Have a solicitor check it before anyone signs (see the notes below). Sign it **before** any contributor starts work. If the designer has already started, sign it now: clause 3 also covers work already done.

**Between:** Abram [surname], London ("the Founder"), for himself and for the company he will incorporate to run the project ("the Company"),
**and** [contributor's full name], [address] ("the Contributor").

**The project:** a day planner that predicts how places and moments will make each person feel, and plans around it. It includes the app, backend, designs, content, data and brand ("the Project").

1. **Role.** The Contributor will act as [designer / safety backup helper / other] and will do the work set out in the Schedule. The Contributor chooses how and when to do the work, within the hours and deadlines agreed in the Schedule. This agreement does not make the Contributor an employee. It gives no pay, only the equity in clause 5. Expenses are repaid only if the Founder agreed to them in writing beforehand.

2. **Hours.** About [x] hours a week, starting on [date] (the "Start Date"). Either side may change the hours by agreeing in writing, and an email is enough.

3. **IP belongs to the Company.** The Contributor assigns to the Founder, with full title guarantee, all copyright, design rights, database rights and other intellectual property in everything they create for the Project (the "Work"). This covers Work done before the date of this agreement, and future Work as it is created (CDPA 1988 s.91). The Founder holds the Work for the Company, and will assign it to the Company once the Company is incorporated. The Contributor will sign any further document needed to confirm this. The Contributor will not use anyone else's material in the Work unless they have the right to do so, and will tell the Founder about any third-party material or licence.

4. **Moral rights.** The Contributor waives their moral rights in the Work (CDPA 1988 s.87), for the Founder, the Company, and their licensees and successors. The Founder will credit the Contributor where it is practical.

5. **Equity (placeholder).** Once the Company is incorporated, the Founder will arrange for the Contributor to be granted **[~10%] of the Company's fully diluted equity**. The form (shares with vesting, or options), the price and the paperwork are to be set with the solicitor and accountant at that time.
   - **Vesting is over 4 years from the Start Date, with a 1-year cliff.** Nothing vests before the first anniversary. On it, 25% vests. The rest vests in equal monthly parts over the next 36 months.
   - If the contribution ends, anything unvested lapses and the Contributor keeps what has vested. The exception is a serious breach of clauses 3, 6 or 7: those "bad leaver" terms are to be set by the solicitor.
   - Nothing is owed if the Company is never incorporated. The Work still belongs to the Founder under clause 3.

6. **Confidentiality.** The Contributor keeps the Project's non-public information confidential, during this agreement and afterwards. This includes plans, code, data and anything about users or participants. They use it only for the Project, and return or delete it when the contribution ends.

7. **Personal data** (this clause matters most for the safety backup helper).
   - The Contributor sees users' or participants' personal data only as needed for their role, and only on the Founder's instructions.
   - They keep it on a device that is locked with a passcode, and never copy it elsewhere.
   - They never use it to contact anyone for other reasons.
   - They tell the Founder within 24 hours of any loss or wrong disclosure, and delete it when the contribution ends.
   - The helper follows the safety procedure (D46): tell the person to call 999 in an emergency, and pass every alert to the Founder.

8. **Authority.** The Contributor can't sign contracts, spend money or make promises for the Founder or the Company.

9. **Ending.** Either side may end this agreement with one week's written notice, and an email is enough. Clauses 3, 4, 5 (for vested equity), 6 and 7 continue after it ends.

10. **Law.** The law of England and Wales governs this agreement, and the courts of England and Wales decide any dispute about it.
