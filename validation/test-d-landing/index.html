A day out that fits you, not a list of sights

Big Ben leaves some people flat. A boat on the Thames, street food and a walk through the park make their day. For someone else it's the opposite. This planner learns which one you are, then plans around it: weather, crowds and travel time included.

How it would work

  1. A short chat about days you loved and ones you didn't, by voice or typing.
  2. A plan for your day, built around what makes you feel good, and explained ("we swapped the long walk for the boat because heat and long transfers wear you out").
  3. One tap after each stop so the next plan fits better.
