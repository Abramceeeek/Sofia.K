How long

  • Audio: deleted on transcription, or after 30 days if you opt in.
  • Study data: deleted by 31 March 2027; anonymised results may be kept.
  • Waitlist emails: kept until launch or until you unsubscribe, whichever is first.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, or object, by contacting abram@abdurakhmonbek.com (or, in the study, by messaging the study's WhatsApp number). We reply within one month. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

No payments

The waitlist takes no money and no card details. If prices are shown, we will email you before anything is ever charged.
