**Who is running this?** Abram, an individual in London, testing an idea for a travel planning app. This is research: nothing is being sold, and there is no payment either way. Contact: abram@abdurakhmonbek.com, or WhatsApp +44 7771 477717 (the study's number).

**What you'll do**
- A 20–30 minute chat on WhatsApp, by voice notes or typing, your choice. We use a separate number just for this study.
- Then two day plans to compare. Optionally, use one on a real day and send a 1–5 rating after each stop.

**If something goes wrong on the day:** in an emergency, call 999. For anything else that feels unsafe, message Abram on the study's WhatsApp number, +44 7771 477717. He will reply within a few hours.

**What is recorded, and why**

| What | Why | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|
| Your answers (text, and transcripts of voice notes) | To understand what makes a day out good for you, and to make your plan | Until 31 March 2027, then deleted. Anonymised results may be kept |
| **Voice notes (audio)** | Only to turn them into text | **Deleted as soon as they are transcribed** (and deleted from the chat on our side; the copy on your own phone is yours to keep or delete), unless you opt in below |
| Your ratings, and the date, time and weather of each stop | To learn what made each stop feel good | Until 31 March 2027 |
| Name and WhatsApp number | To contact you during the study | Deleted when the study ends |

- **Transcription** happens on Abram's laptop. Your audio is not sent to any transcription or AI company. WhatsApp itself carries the messages, and its own privacy policy applies to them.
- **AI help:** Abram uses an AI service (Claude, made by Anthropic) to help write up your summary and to run the program that makes your plans. It only ever gets your answers under a code like P07: never your name, number or voice notes. Abram checks everything it writes. The privacy notice below says how Anthropic handles it.

**Optional:** if you're happy for Abram to keep your voice notes for **30 days** to check the transcripts are accurate, add "and keep my voice notes" to your reply. They are then deleted. (Saying no changes nothing else.)

**What we don't ask for:** we don't need your nationality, health details or allergies. Please don't send them. If you do, they won't be recorded.

**Your choices**
- Taking part is voluntary.
- Skip any question.
- Stop at any time without giving a reason.
- Ask to see or delete your data at any time: message Abram or email abram@abdurakhmonbek.com. We'll delete it within 14 days.

**Is it a real person?** Yes. Abram runs the chat. AI only helps write up the summary and run the planning program.

**To take part, reply "I agree"** in the chat. By replying, you confirm that you're 18 or over, that you've read this form and the privacy notice below, and that you agree to take part.
