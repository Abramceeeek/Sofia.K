# Three independent reviews of the London examples (24 Nov 2026)

`examples.md` was reviewed by three separate reviewers, each from a different viewpoint:
- the traveller;
- a London guide (who also read all 49 ready cards);
- a UX writer.

The reviewers were AI subagents with no part in building the plans. They read the files and changed nothing.

**These are opinions from software, not evidence about real travellers.** Their factual claims about places need checking on official pages before anything is changed. Two were checked by one reviewer, both on 24 Nov 2026:
- the Santander Cycles age rule, from the app listing (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id974792287);
- Horizon 22's booking, from Visit London (https://www.visitlondon.com/things-to-do/place/50158902-horizon-22).

## What's good (keep it)

- **Exact ways in:** "go down from the glass pavilion beside it, by the steps or the lift".
- **Descriptions that sell the moment:** "St Paul's dome sits dead ahead, growing with every step".
- **Honest friction:** "the last part uphill", "an airport-style security check".
- **Close, real rain options.**
- **Disliked places themselves kept out** (no British Museum for Sam, no Camden for those who disliked it).
- **Sam's second day:** compact, named and walkable.

## The three reviewers' conditions for "yes" (from the London guide)

1. Fill the person's whole window: no silent wait over 20 minutes, evenings for night people, and the galleries' Friday lates on their cards.
2. Enforce what the cards already say (age, booking, kit, season, the true best time) before a plan is shown.
3. About 30–40 more checked cards: the swipe places, named food by cuisine and price in each area, and sights for children and big history. Then rerun these six travellers. **Each person's "one change" should now happen without being asked.**
