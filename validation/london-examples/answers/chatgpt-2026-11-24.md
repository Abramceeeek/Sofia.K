## Part B

**B1. Best things:**
1. Place descriptions (St Paul's dome growing across the Millennium Bridge).
2. Practical arrival instructions (the glass pavilion at St Martin-in-the-Fields).
3. Some genuine personalisation (Sam's Brick Lane bagel, Priya and Dan's British Museum, the family's Greenwich view).

**B2. Five problems, ranked:**
1. **The planner doesn't fill the requested day.** All 13 days finish at least an hour early, some nearly four. *Fix:* plan against the whole window; the number of attractions must not be the stopping condition.
2. **Unexplained gaps** (the friends 14:21–15:30 after a four-minute walk; Priya and Dan 13:59–15:30; Margaret and Alan 14:12–15:30). *Fix:* schedule backwards from fixed-time experiences; no unexplained gap over 20 minutes; show real free time.
3. **Preferences aren't strong enough constraints** (Jonas; the Uber Boat for the family and for Margaret and Alan). *Fix:* require a stop matching a loved experience or its defining features; penalise disliked features, not only names.
4. **Real-world suitability isn't enforced** (the bike for an eight-year-old; booking, kit, season, weather). *Fix:* hard-check age, booking, equipment, season and access before showing a plan.
5. **Explanations and food aren't trustworthy enough** (the lido's 06:30–10:00 "best time" on an afternoon visit; "Pick a café, pub or food stall nearby"). *Fix:*
   - one genuine Why per stop, with no internal card-window lines;
   - named food places, and lunch at sensible times;
   - evening food for night people;
   - costs per person and for the party.

**B3. Wrong or risky:**
- the lido's best time;
- the Garden at 120's flowers in December;
- the bike's age rule;
- Jonas's rooftop garden;
- two towers within minutes for the friends;
- no return journey shown to Charing Cross;
- family costs, each or total;
- Somerset House's courtyard in December.

**B4. Missing:**
- **Sam:** markets, cheap cuisine-specific places, evening street food.
- **Priya and Dan:** major history sights, Friday lates.
- **Family:** places for children, indoor rainy-day options, boats.
- **Jonas:** canal walks, Primrose Hill.
- **Friends:** Camden, the cable car, evenings, pubs, music.
- **Margaret and Alan:** the Uber Boat, modern art, the river.
- **For everyone:** a convincing evening programme.

**B5. Compared with a well-informed London friend: 3/10.** "ODYS currently writes about individual places better than it connects them into a coherent experience."

**B6. Would recommend: not yet.** "The next release should prioritise planner correctness rather than adding more cities."

## ChatGPT's recommendation

Treat planner correctness as a **release blocker**, with these acceptance tests:
1. no unexplained gap over 20 minutes;
2. no day ending more than 45 minutes early;
3. every traveller gets an experience reflecting a loved place;
4. disliked experience types are actively excluded;
5. age, booking and equipment constraints pass before a plan is made;
6. every Why is consistent with the actual stop.

These six became the London gate, `scripts/london_gate.py` (m326).
