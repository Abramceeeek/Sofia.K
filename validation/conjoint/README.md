# Test A "this or that" design

**Status: proposed. Abram to confirm the attributes and levels** (LOG open question). About 1 h to review. Rerun the script after any change, which takes about 1 minute.

- **Ten paired day plans (A or B)**, generated by `make_design.py` with a fixed seed. Output: `design.csv`.
- **Bayesian D-efficient** for a binary logit with weak priors (Area 3). Coordinate exchange from 6 random starts; best Bayesian D-error 0.695.
- **Checks built in:**
  - the two plans in a task differ on at least 3 attributes;
  - neither plan dominates on the attributes whose sign is known (transfer, heat, crowds, cost);
  - levels are balanced (2-level attributes 10/10; transfer 7/7/6; cost 6/8/6).
- **Fixed, not adaptive.** Adaptive designs hurt population estimates (Area 3). The same 10 tasks go to every participant.

| Attribute | Levels shown | Coding | Prior mean (sd 0.5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transfer | longest transfer 15 / 30 / 50 min | dummies vs 15 | −0.3, −1.0 (a threshold, not a line) |
| Heat | no hot hour / one hot hour outside (28°C+) | dummy | −0.5 |
| Pace | 3 stops, relaxed / 5 stops, packed | dummy | 0 (people differ) |
| Crowds | mostly quiet / busy at 2 stops | dummy | −0.4 |
| Cost | £20 / £40 / £60 per person | linear per £10 | −0.25 |
| Walking | about 3 km / about 8 km | dummy | 0 (people differ) |

**What 12 participants give:**
- 12 × 10 = 120 choices is enough for a pooled logit of these 7 parameters, a sanity check on signs and rough magnitudes.
- Individual βs need the Phase 4 hierarchical model plus swipes. Ten choices only roughly halve individual error (Area 3).

**Interactions (heat × walking)** can't be identified per person from 10 choices. Estimate them at population level later.
