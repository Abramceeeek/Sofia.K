# Test A "this or that" design

**Status: confirmed by Abram (D30, D95, 28 Sep).** The heat factor is replaced by a 3-level weather factor. Rerun the script after any change; it takes about 3 minutes (`--report` adds the efficiency checks).

- **Ten paired day plans (A or B)**, generated by `make_design.py` with a fixed seed. Output: `design.csv`, copied to `brain/data/conjoint_design.csv`.
- **Bayesian D-efficient** for a binary logit with weak priors (Area 3). Coordinate exchange from 6 random starts; best Bayesian D-error 0.852 (8 parameters).
- **Checks built in:**
  - the two plans in a task differ on at least 3 attributes;
  - neither plan dominates on the attributes whose sign is known (transfer, weather, crowds, cost). Weather is ordered mild and dry, then cold but dry, then rainy;
  - levels are balanced: 2-level attributes 10/10; transfer 7/7/6; weather 6/7/7; cost 5/7/8;
  - every weather pair appears: mild vs cold 3, mild vs rainy 3, cold vs rainy 4.
- **Fixed, not adaptive.** Adaptive designs hurt population estimates (Area 3). The same 10 tasks go to every participant.

| Attribute | Levels shown | Coding | Prior mean (sd 0.5) | Brain feature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Transfer | longest transfer 15 / 30 / 50 min | dummies vs 15 | −0.3, −1.0 (a threshold, not a line) | `transfer_15_30`, `transfer_30_plus` |
| **Weather at the outdoor stops (D95)** | mild and dry / cold but dry / rainy | dummies vs mild and dry | cold −0.5, rain −1.0 | `cold_outdoor` (8°C or below × outdoors), `rain_outdoor` (rain × outdoors) |
| Pace | 3 stops, relaxed / 5 stops, packed | dummy | 0 (people differ) | `packed_pace` |
| Crowds | mostly quiet / busy at 2 stops | dummy | −0.4 | `crowds` |
| Cost | £20 / £40 / £60 per person | linear per £10 | −0.25 | `cost_per_10gbp` (log scale, D88) |
| Walking | about 3 km / about 8 km | dummy | 0 (people differ) | `long_walk` |

**If a participant asks what the weather levels mean:** mild and dry is about 15°C with no rain; cold but dry is about 5°C with no rain; rainy is showers on and off. The model's cold threshold is 8°C (GUESS).

## Is 10 tasks still efficient? (checked 28 Sep, `make_design.py --report`)

**Yes (my judgement): keep 10.**
- **Close to the best possible for 10 tasks:** a 20-start search found a D-error only 0.5% lower (0.848 against 0.852).
- **The five factors you kept lose little precision.** Expected standard errors for the pooled 12 participants × 10 tasks, at the prior means:

  | Parameter | Old design (heat) | New design (weather) |
  |---|---|---|
  | transfer 30 | 0.27 | 0.27 |
  | transfer 50 | 0.32 | 0.35 |
  | pace | 0.21 | 0.20 |
  | crowds | 0.21 | 0.22 |
  | cost per £10 | 0.10 | 0.12 |
  | walking | 0.21 | 0.21 |
  | heat → **cold / rain** | 0.21 | **0.31 / 0.37** |

- **The cost is on weather:** two contrasts share the same 10 tasks. Pooled, rain is clear (prior −1.0 against SE 0.37), but cold is borderline (−0.5 against 0.31).
- **Per person,** weather weights stay mostly on the prior (SE about 1.1 from 10 choices), as before. Swipes and later ratings refine them.
- **If you want more:** 12 tasks lower the D-error by 19%, for about 1 extra minute per participant (2 more A/B messages). That's your call; it isn't needed for Test B.

**What 12 participants give:**
- 12 × 10 = 120 choices is enough for a pooled logit of these 8 parameters, a sanity check on signs and rough magnitudes.
- Individual βs need the Phase 4 hierarchical model plus swipes.

**Interactions (heat × walking)** can't be identified per person from 10 choices. Estimate them at population level later. Heat is no longer in the design; `hot_outdoor` keeps its prior and learns from stop ratings.
