# Test B dry run (D86, D97, D100): read these as a participant would

*Round 2, regenerated 28 Sep after the friends' feedback (D100). **Two made-up people, not participants**, built from the real results-sheet template. Brief: a Saturday, from London Bridge station, **10:00–15:00**, 4 stops, £40 (`for-abram/brief.used.yaml`). The personal plans come from the ready cards, with the familiar-favourite rule switched on (`--familiar`, proposed in D100). The generic plan is the new "best of London" dry-run copy (`for-abram/generic.used.yaml`); the real one is yours to write to the same standard (0.5 h, week of 19 Oct).*

## Round 1 feedback (6 friends, 3 per person; for fixes only, not test data)
- **6 of 6 preferred the personal plan, and 6 of 6 spotted it**, all from how well the places fitted the person, none from the format. Read this as "blindness held, personalisation visible".
- **Caveats:**
  - The persona lines listed likes and dislikes openly, which makes the personal plan easier to spot than it will be for a real participant.
  - The generic plan read as a weak "crowded tourist checklist", and P02's started with Big Ben, a stated dislike. A strong generic plan is the fair comparison, so it was rewritten.

## What changed for round 2 (D100)
1. **Generic plan:** a strong "best of London" day: Borough Market, Tate Modern with its free Level 10 view, over the Millennium Bridge to a free roof terrace by St Paul's, then the South Bank past the London Eye.
2. **Familiar favourites (proposed; your call):** the personal plan holds exactly one place the person loved, if one fits the day, and every other stop is new. Places they didn't enjoy are never planned. The review explains each loved place: in the plan, or why not (no card yet, hours, budget, distance, or the one-favourite rule).
3. **Costs:** "about £X for food and entry, plus about £Y transport". Food is never called "entry". Transport is about £3 per Tube or bus leg, capped at £8.90 a day.
4. **Times:** every stop shows arrive–leave.
5. **"Best: …"** appears only when the stop's time matches (e.g. "Sunset" only on a sunset stop).
6. **Travel legs:**
   - the St James's Park → Buckingham Palace walk is gone with the old generic plan;
   - Greenwich Park now starts at Cutty Sark DLR, with the 15–20 min uphill walk in the text and the timing;
   - the pier is mentioned only on the boat card.
7. **Rain:** an "If it rains: …" swap for outdoor stops (an indoor or covered moment nearby, open at that time). The review flags a plan with none.
8. **Wording:**
   - both plans open with "Start: London Bridge station at 10:00";
   - every arrival line is a full sentence;
   - arrival and description lines no longer repeat each other, and the review checks for repeats;
   - the London Eye is a walk-past, not a ride.
9. **Buffer:** 5 min after each walk and 10 min after each Tube or bus leg.
10. **Real Test B script:** ask for a gut reaction, not an analysis, and mark long, polished answers as possibly AI-assisted (`validation/interview-script.md`).

**Ends:** P01 at 14:10 and P02 at 14:14, against the generic plan's 14:40 (both within 30 min).

**The real Test B runs 12:00–17:00 (D99),** so its day ends around sunset. This dry run stays at 10:00–15:00.

## Sharing with friends
- **Give each friend one person's pair,** P01 or P02. Both people get the same generic plan, so a friend who reads all four can tell which plan is generic.
- **Friends' feedback is for catching problems only, not test data.** It is never entered in the results sheet, and friends don't count towards Test B's numbers.
- **Useful questions:**
  - What is confusing?
  - What is wrong (a time, a route, a price, an opening time)?
  - What would you change?
