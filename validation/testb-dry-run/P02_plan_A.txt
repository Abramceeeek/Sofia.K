*Plan A: A Saturday out in London*
10:00–18:00 · 4 stops · entry costs about £22.50 per person

*1. 10:06  Graze your way round Borough Market*
Getting there: about 6 min on foot from London Bridge station.
From London Bridge station. The market is right next to London Bridge station
You taste your way round the stalls, pick a few things, and eat standing up among the traders.
Cost: £15

*2. 11:27  A rooftop garden among the City towers*
Getting there: about 16 min on foot from Borough Market.
From Bank, Monument or Fenchurch Street station. Enter from Fenchurch Street or Fenchurch Avenue through Hogarth Court, pass an airport-style security check, and take the lift to floor 15
You step out of a lift 15 floors up into a garden of wisteria, roses and hydrangeas around a water feature, with the City's towers all around and views to the Thames and Tower Bridge.
Best: Daylight; the towers light up at dusk
Cost: free

*3. 12:24  A late bagel on Brick Lane*
Getting there: about 22 min by Tube or bus from The Garden at 120.
From Liverpool Street or Aldgate East station. Walk up Brick Lane to number 159
You queue at a counter that never closes and eat a hot salt beef bagel with mustard on the pavement.
Cost: £7.50

*4. 13:28  Canal steps and Coal Drops Yard at King's Cross*
Getting there: about 32 min by Tube or bus from Beigel Bake.
From King's Cross St Pancras station. Walk north from the station up King's Boulevard to Granary Square
You sit on the wide steps down to Regent's Canal, walk through the fountains in Granary Square, and wander under the curved roofs of Coal Drops Yard, among the new tech offices.
Best: Daylight for the fountains; the yard is lit in the evening
Cost: free
