*Plan 2: A Saturday out in London*
10:00–15:00 · 4 stops · no entry costs

*1. 10:30  Big Ben and Parliament Square*
Getting there: about 20 min by Tube or bus from London Bridge station.
Take the Jubilee line from London Bridge to Westminster, then walk to the square.
The clock tower and the Houses of Parliament from the square.
Best: Morning, before the tour groups
Cost: free

*2. 11:15  Along the South Bank to the London Eye*
Getting there: about 10 min on foot from Parliament Square.
From Parliament Square, cross Westminster Bridge.
Cross Westminster Bridge and walk the river path.
Best: Late morning, with the river busy
Cost: free

*3. 12:40  Trafalgar Square*
Getting there: about 20 min on foot from the London Eye.
From the London Eye, cross Hungerford Bridge and walk up to the square.
Nelson's Column and the fountains.
Best: Any time
Cost: free

*4. 13:35  St James's Park to Buckingham Palace*
Getting there: about 10 min on foot from Trafalgar Square.
From Trafalgar Square, go through Admiralty Arch into the park.
Walk through the park to the palace gates.
Best: Early afternoon
Cost: free
