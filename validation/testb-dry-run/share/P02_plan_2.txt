*Plan 2: A Saturday out in London*
Start: London Bridge station at 10:00 · 4 stops, including lunch
Costs: about £20 for food and entry, all on foot

*1. 10:05–10:45  Borough Market*
Getting there: about 5 min on foot from London Bridge station.
Walk into the market; it sits right beside the station.
Browse the food stalls and pick up a coffee and something small to eat.
Food: about £5

*2. 11:00–12:15  Tate Modern: the Turbine Hall and the view from Level 10*
Getting there: about 15 min on foot from Borough Market.
Walk west along Bankside, past Shakespeare's Globe.
Walk into the vast Turbine Hall, look round the free collection, then take the lift from Level 1 up to Level 10 for a view over the Thames to St Paul's.
Cost: free

*3. 12:25–13:15  Lunch by the river on the South Bank*
Getting there: about 10 min on foot from Tate Modern.
Walk west along the river path towards the Oxo Tower.
Pick a café or restaurant along the river for lunch.
Food: about £15

*4. 13:25–14:40  The South Bank, past the London Eye*
Getting there: about 5 min on foot from the Oxo Tower.
Carry on west along the river path.
Walk past the National Theatre and the Southbank Centre to the London Eye, with the river and the Houses of Parliament across the water. You walk past the London Eye; a ride isn't part of this plan.
Cost: free
If it rains: The Royal Festival Hall's free foyers, on the way (closed on Mondays).
