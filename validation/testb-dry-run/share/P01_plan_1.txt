*Plan 1: A Saturday out in London*
Start: London Bridge station at 10:00 · 4 stops
Costs: about £15 for food and entry, all on foot

*1. 10:05–11:00  Breakfast at Borough Market*
Getting there: about 5 min on foot from London Bridge station.
Walk into the market; it sits right beside the station.
Graze the food stalls: pick a few things and eat as you wander.
Food: about £15

*2. 11:15–12:25  Tate Modern: the Turbine Hall and the view from Level 10*
Getting there: about 12 min on foot from Borough Market.
Walk west along Bankside, past Shakespeare's Globe.
Walk into the vast Turbine Hall, look round the free collection, then take the lift from Level 1 up to Level 10 for a view over the Thames to St Paul's.
Cost: free

*3. 12:35–13:25  Over the Millennium Bridge to St Paul's and a free roof terrace*
Getting there: about 3 min on foot from Tate Modern.
Cross the Millennium Bridge towards the dome.
Walk round St Paul's Cathedral, then go up to the free roof terrace of One New Change, just east of it, for a close view of the dome.
Cost: free
If it rains: Stay longer inside Tate Modern instead, and cross the bridge later.

*4. 13:40–14:40  Along the South Bank to the London Eye*
Getting there: about 12 min on foot from One New Change.
Cross Blackfriars Bridge to the South Bank.
Walk west along the river past the Oxo Tower, the National Theatre and the Southbank Centre. You walk past the London Eye; a ride isn't part of this plan.
Cost: free
If it rains: The Royal Festival Hall's free foyers, on the way (closed on Mondays).
