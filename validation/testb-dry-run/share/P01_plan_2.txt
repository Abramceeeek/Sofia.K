*Plan 2: A Saturday out in London*
10:00–15:00 · 4 stops · no entry costs

*1. 10:26  Across the Millennium Bridge towards St Paul's dome*
Getting there: about 16 min on foot from London Bridge station.
From Tate Modern's river side, step onto the south end of the bridge and walk north.
You walk north across the steel footbridge, and St Paul's dome sits dead ahead, growing with every step.
Best: Any time; at dusk the dome is lit
Cost: free

*2. 11:11  A ruined church garden hidden in the City*
Getting there: about 20 min on foot from St Paul's Cathedral.
Walk down St Dunstan's Hill towards the river.
Among office blocks, you walk into the ruins of a medieval church that is now a public garden.
Best: Daylight
Cost: free

*3. 11:50  A rooftop garden among the City towers*
Getting there: about 5 min on foot from St Dunstan-in-the-East.
Enter from Fenchurch Street or Fenchurch Avenue through Hogarth Court, pass an airport-style security check, and take the lift to floor 15.
You step out of a lift 15 floors up into a garden of wisteria, roses and hydrangeas around a water feature, with the City's towers all around and views to the Thames and Tower Bridge.
Best: Daylight; the towers light up at dusk
Cost: free

*4. 13:15  The whole of London from General Wolfe's hill, Greenwich Park*
Getting there: about 41 min by Tube or bus from The Garden at 120.
From Cutty Sark DLR or Greenwich Pier. Walk into the park and climb the hill to the statue beside the Royal Observatory.
You climb through the park to the statue, and all of London opens up across the river: Canary Wharf, the Shard and St Paul's.
Best: A clear day; late-afternoon light
Cost: free
