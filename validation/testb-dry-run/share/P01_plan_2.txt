*Plan 2: A Saturday out in London*
Start: London Bridge station at 10:00 · 4 stops
Costs: free entry, plus about £3 transport

*1. 10:23–10:58  A rooftop garden among the City towers*
Getting there: about 15 min on foot from London Bridge station.
Go into Hogarth Court from Fenchurch Street or Fenchurch Avenue, pass an airport-style security check, and take the lift to floor 15.
You step out 15 floors up into a garden of wisteria, roses and hydrangeas around a water feature, with the City's towers all around and views to the Thames and Tower Bridge.
Cost: free
If it rains: Graze your way round Borough Market, about 15 min away on foot.

*2. 11:16–11:41  A ruined church garden hidden in the City*
Getting there: about 5 min on foot from The Garden at 120.
Walk down St Dunstan's Hill towards the river.
Among office blocks, you walk into the ruins of a medieval church that is now a public garden.
Cost: free

*3. 12:14–12:30  Across the Millennium Bridge towards St Paul's dome*
Getting there: about 20 min on foot from St Dunstan-in-the-East.
From Tate Modern's river side, step onto the south end of the bridge and walk north.
You walk north across the steel footbridge, and St Paul's dome sits dead ahead, growing with every step.
Cost: free

*4. 13:24–14:10  A park break: the Serpentine in Hyde Park*
Getting there: about 37 min by Tube or bus from St Paul's Cathedral.
From Hyde Park Corner or Knightsbridge station, walk into the park.
Walk into the park to the Serpentine and slow down by the water.
Cost: free
If it rains: Into the whale hall (Natural History Museum), about 15 min away on foot.
