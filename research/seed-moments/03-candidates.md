## How they're ranked: fit with the Test B brief

Test B compares a personal plan with a good generic top-attractions plan, for strangers, in the weeks of 19 Oct – 9 Nov. So a candidate ranks higher when it:
1. **Can be planned in those weeks.** It's open, has fixed hours, and needs no booking weeks ahead. The clocks go back on 25 Oct, and it gets dark around 16:30 (GUESS).
2. **Fills a gap:**

   - the thin tags: **absorbing and active** moments, **food**, and **area vibes**.
3. **Splits people.** It isn't on the generic plan, so personal and generic plans can differ.
4. **Is central, cheap and combinable:** zones 1–2, under £20, and near other cards.
5. **Is low-risk for strangers.**

## Tier A

**1. Bank of England Museum: lift a real gold bar**
- **Where / arrive:** Bank of England, Threadneedle Street*. Bank station.
- **What happens:** You walk through the story of money and the Bank. At the end, you put your arm through a secure hatch and lift a real gold bar of about 13 kg.
- **Why it works:** The weight in your hand makes finance physical. It's surprising, and people talk about it afterwards.
- **Best time:** Mon–Fri 10:00–17:00, and to 20:00 on the third Thursday. Free, no booking. Weekday mornings are likely quieter (GUESS).
- **Sources:** [Wikivoyage: City](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/London/City); [ianVisits venue page](https://www.ianvisits.co.uk/venues/bank-of-england-museum/)

**2. The Garden at 120: a rooftop garden among the towers**
- **Where / arrive:** Fen Court, 120 Fenchurch Street. Enter from Fenchurch Street or Fenchurch Avenue via Hogarth Court, then a short security check and a lift to floor 15. Bank, Monument or Fenchurch Street*.
- **What happens:** You step out of a lift into wisteria, fruit trees and a long water channel, with the City's towers all around you.
- **Why it works:** A garden where you expect glass and steel. It's both calm and the heart of the finance district.
- **Best time:** Winter hours (from 1 Oct): daily 10:00–18:30, weekends to 17:00; closed on bank holidays. Free, no booking (except groups of 12+). No alcohol.
- **Source:** [fen-court.com](https://fen-court.com/garden-at-120/)

**3. Leadenhall Market: in through a passage**
- **Where / arrive:** In the City, between Gracechurch Street and Lime Street*. Bank or Monument*.
- **What happens:** You slip down a narrow lane and come out under a high Victorian glass-and-iron roof, painted green, maroon and cream, over cobbles (Horace Jones, 1881).
- **Why it works:** Compression, then release. On weekdays the City's lunchtime pace fills it; the energy belongs to the place, not to any one crowd.
- **Best time:** Weekdays 12:00–14:00 for the buzz. The public areas are open 24/7; shops keep weekday hours.
- **Sources:** [leadenhallmarket.co.uk](https://leadenhallmarket.co.uk/); [history page](https://leadenhallmarket.co.uk/history-of-leadenhall-market-discover-our-heritage/)

**4. Borough Market: graze a lunch**
- **Where / arrive:** By London Bridge station.
- **What happens:** You taste your way round the stalls and eat standing up under the railway.
- **Why it works:** Food as the experience, not a pit stop. It's also on the swipe page, so reactions to it are measured.
- **Best time:** Tue–Fri 10:00–17:00, Sat 09:00–17:00, Sun 10:00–16:00; closed Mondays (open daily in December). Late weekday mornings are calmer (GUESS).
- **Source:** [boroughmarket.org.uk](https://boroughmarket.org.uk/visit-us/)

**5. Maltby Street Market (weekends)**
- **Where / arrive:** Ropewalk, Bermondsey, along the 19th-century railway arches. London Bridge or Bermondsey*.
- **What happens:** A narrow lane of food stalls, smaller and more local than Borough Market.
- **Why it works:** It feels found rather than famous. It pairs with Shad Thames (10) and go-karting (6).
- **Best time:** Sat 10:00–17:00, Sun 11:00–16:00.
- **Source:** [maltbystreetmarket.co.uk](http://www.maltbystreetmarket.co.uk/)

**6. Indoor go-karting at Tower Bridge**
- **Where / arrive:** TeamSport, Tower Bridge Business Park, 100 Clements Road SE16. A short walk from Bermondsey station.
- **What happens:** You're kitted out in a suit and helmet, then race electric karts round a two-level indoor track with up to 20 drivers.
- **Why it works:** Total absorption: there's no room to think about work. It's the go-karting you added to the swipe page, and it's strongly social.
- **Best time:** Daily 09:00–23:00. Timed sessions from £20 per person (search results; check before drafting).
- **Source:** [TeamSport London tracks](https://www.team-sport.co.uk/go-karting-london)

**7. Millennium Bridge towards St Paul's dome**
- **Where / arrive:** Start at Tate Modern on Bankside. Southwark or Blackfriars.
- **What happens:** You walk north across the steel footbridge, and St Paul's dome sits dead ahead, growing with every step.
- **Why it works:** A framed reveal, from new London to old London. It links to evensong (9) and the City cluster.
- **Best time:** Any time. It's always open and free. Dusk adds the lights.
- **Source:** [London Museum: the Millennium Bridge](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/collections/london-stories/london-millennium-bridge/)

**8. Waterloo Bridge at sunset**
- **Where / arrive:** The middle of Waterloo Bridge, from Waterloo station or the Strand*.
- **What happens:** Face west for Parliament and the London Eye, then cross the road and face east for St Paul's and the City's glass towers catching the light.
- **Why it works:** Two of London's great views, a road's width apart, and it's free. Late-autumn sunsets are the best of the year for it.
- **Best time:** Sunset, around 16:30 after 25 Oct (GUESS).
- **Source:** [South Bank: best sunset views](https://southbank.london/guides/south-banks-best-sunset-views)

**9. Choral evensong at St Paul's**
- **Where / arrive:** St Paul's Cathedral. St Paul's station.
- **What happens:** You sit under the dome for about 45 minutes while the choir sings, as the light outside goes.
- **Why it works:** Awe and stillness in one of London's great rooms, for free. Offer it as music and architecture: the app must never use it to guess anyone's religion (a protected characteristic).
- **Best time:** Mon–Sat 17:00, Sun 15:00. Arrive about 15 minutes early.
- **Source:** [St Paul's: Choral Evensong](https://www.stpauls.co.uk/whats-on/choral-evensong-sunday)

**10. Shad Thames under the iron gantries**
- **Where / arrive:** Start at the south end of Tower Bridge. London Bridge or Tower Hill*.
- **What happens:** You turn off the riverside into a cobbled canyon of 1870s spice-and-tea warehouses, under iron walkways that cross overhead, and follow it to St Saviour's Dock.
- **Why it works:** A sudden Victorian world a minute from the crowds on the bridge. The history is in the fabric of the street.
- **Best time:** Any time; free. Daylight or blue hour.
- **Source:** [Wikipedia: Shad Thames](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shad_Thames)

**11. Greenwich Park: the view from General Wolfe**
- **Where / arrive:** The top of the hill by the Royal Observatory. From Cutty Sark DLR or Greenwich Pier.
- **What happens:** You climb through the park to the statue, and the whole of London opens up across the river: Canary Wharf, the Shard and St Paul's.
- **Why it works:** Nature, history and a skyline together. It's a natural start before the day river trip (card 3).
- **Best time:** The park opens at 06:00 and closes as early as 18:00 in winter.
- **Sources:** [Royal Parks: Greenwich Park monuments](https://www.royalparks.org.uk/visit/parks/greenwich-park/monuments); [Royal Museums Greenwich](https://www.rmg.co.uk/attractions/greenwich-park)

**12. St Dunstan-in-the-East: a ruined church garden**
- **Where / arrive:** St Dunstan's Hill, between the Monument and Tower Hill.
- **What happens:** Among office blocks, you walk into the ruins of a medieval church that is now a public garden.
- **Why it works:** A strong contrast, green and quiet, in the heart of the City.
- **Best time:** 08:00 to 19:00 or dusk, whichever is earlier (Wikivoyage; check). Free.
- **Source:** [Wikivoyage: City](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/London/City)

**13. Parliament Hill view**
- **Where / arrive:** Hampstead Heath, from Gospel Oak* or Hampstead Heath* stations.
- **What happens:** You walk up through the heath, and at the top the skyline lies spread out below: St Paul's, the Shard and the City.
- **Why it works:** A legally protected view from 98 m, with open heath all around. Real nature, with the city as the backdrop.
- **Best time:** A clear late afternoon. Free.
- **Source:** [City of London: Parliament Hill viewpoint](https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/things-to-do/green-spaces/hampstead-heath/where-to-go-at-hampstead-heath/parliament-hill-viewpoint)

**14. Horizon 22: level 58, free**
- **Where / arrive:** 22 Bishopsgate. Liverpool Street or Bank*.
- **What happens:** A fast lift takes you to level 58 (254 m) and a 300° view over the City towers and beyond.
- **Why it works:** Height and awe, inside the finance district itself.
- **Best time:** Mon–Fri 10:00–18:00 (last arrival about 16:30–17:00), Sat 10:00–17:00, Sun 10:00–16:00. Free, but book online; there are a few walk-in places. Near sunset on a weekday (GUESS).
- **Source:** [horizon22.co.uk: plan your visit](https://horizon22.co.uk/visit)

**15. King's Cross: canal steps and Coal Drops Yard** *(area vibe)*
- **Where / arrive:** Granary Square, a few minutes' walk north of King's Cross station.
- **What happens:** You sit on the wide steps down to Regent's Canal, walk through the fountains, and wander under the two Victorian coal warehouses whose roofs curve to meet (Heatherwick, 2018).
- **Why it works:** Old industry remade. Google and Meta have offices here, so it fits finance & tech as an area, not a single stop.
- **Best time:** Daylight, when the fountains run. Free.
- **Source:** [kingscross.co.uk: Granary Square](https://www.kingscross.co.uk/granary-square)

**16. A late-night bagel on Brick Lane**
- **Where / arrive:** Beigel Bake, 159 Brick Lane. Liverpool Street or Aldgate East.
- **What happens:** You queue at a counter that never closes and eat a fresh bagel on the pavement.
- **Why it works:** A cheap, local food ritual that suits a late finish.
- **Best time:** Open 24 hours (Wikivoyage; check).
- **Source:** [Wikivoyage: East End](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/London/East_End)

## Tier B

**17. Climb the Monument.** 311 steps up the column for the Great Fire, then a caged gallery high over the City. Effort, then reward.
- **Arrive:** Monument station.
- **Hours:** daily 09:30–13:00 and 14:00–18:00 (last entry 12:30 and 17:30).
- **Cost:** £7, paid on the day, valid to 31 Mar 2027.
- **Why B:** it's close to card 7's feeling of height, and the stairs aren't for everyone.
- **Source:** [themonument.org.uk](https://www.themonument.org.uk/plan-your-visit)

**18. Kyoto Garden, Holland Park.** A tiered waterfall, koi, stone lanterns and wandering peacocks; the garden was opened in 1991.
- **Arrive:** Holland Park*.
- **Hours:** the park is open 07:30 to 30 minutes before dusk. Free.
- **Why B:** it's west, away from the other clusters. Autumn colour may be at its best in the Test B weeks (GUESS).
- **Source:** [Visit London: Kyoto Garden guide](https://www.visitlondon.com/blog/kyoto-garden-london-guide)

**19. A heated outdoor swim at London Fields Lido.** A 50 m pool heated to 25 °C all year and floodlit after dark: warm water, cold air, open sky.
- **Arrive:** London Fields*.
- **Booking:** essential. About £5 (a third-party figure; UNVERIFIED).
- **Why B:** you need swim kit, and it strongly splits people.
- **Source:** [Better: London Fields Lido](https://www.better.org.uk/leisure-centre/london/hackney/london-fields-lido)

**20. Mercato Metropolitano.** A food hall in a former paper factory at Elephant & Castle, with 40+ traders.
- **Hours:** Mon–Wed 12:00–23:00, Thu to 24:00, Fri to 01:00, Sat 11:00–01:00, Sun 11:00–22:00.
- **Why B:** good for evenings and rain, but less of a moment than 4 or 5.
- **Source:** [mercatometropolitano.com](https://mercatometropolitano.com/locations/elephant-and-castle/)

**21. Daunt Books' oak gallery.** You walk through a normal shop front into a long Edwardian gallery of oak, with skylights and a stained-glass window; it's a 1912 building.
- **Where:** 83 Marylebone High Street; Baker Street*.
- **Hours:** Mon–Sat 09:00–19:30, Sun 11:00–18:00. Free to browse.
- **Why B:** a strong reveal, but a niche one.
- **Source:** [Wikipedia: Daunt Books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daunt_Books)

**22. Tate Modern level 10 viewing floor.** A free lift in the Blavatnik Building to a 360° view over the Thames.
- **Hours:** about 10:00–18:00, later on some evenings (sources disagree; UNVERIFIED).
- **Why B:** signs ask visitors not to photograph the neighbouring flats, so its photo value is lower than it looks.
- **Source:** [ianVisits: the viewing gallery reopens](https://www.ianvisits.co.uk/articles/tate-modern-reopens-its-10th-floor-viewing-gallery-65498/)

**23. Mail Rail under the Postal Museum.** A 15-minute ride on small trains through the old Post Office railway tunnels.
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00, last entry 15:30.
- **Cost:** about £18.50 in advance (varies; UNVERIFIED).
- **Why B:** novel and absorbing, but dearer and near nothing else on the list.
- **Source:** [Postal Museum tickets](https://www.postalmuseum.org/visit-us/tickets/)

**24. Brixton Village arcades.** Two-storey glass-roofed arcades packed with food from around the world.
- **Hours:** 08:00–23:30 daily, except Mondays (to 18:00).
- **Why B:** zone 2, further out, but a strong food-and-area vibe.
- **Source:** [Brixton Market Traders Federation](https://brixtonmarket.net/brixton-village-and-market-row/)

**25. Southbank Centre Food Market.** Stalls behind the Royal Festival Hall.
- **Hours:** Fri 12:00–20:00, Sat 11:00–20:00, Sun 12:00–18:00 (Wikivoyage; check).
- **Why B:** pairs with Waterloo Bridge at sunset (8), but it's weekend-only.
- **Source:** [Wikivoyage: South Bank](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/London/South_Bank)

**26. Leake Street graffiti tunnel.** A legal graffiti tunnel under Waterloo, with street art there since 2008.
- **Hours:** always open. Free.
- **Why B:** vivid colour and contrast, but some people find it grim, so it splits people.
- **Source:** [Wikivoyage: South Bank](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/London/South_Bank)

**27. A climbing taster in a Victorian "castle".** The Castle Climbing Centre is inside a Grade II listed pumping station from 1854. A one-hour taster tries both bouldering and ropes.
- **Arrive:** Manor House*.
- **Price:** not found (UNVERIFIED).
- **Why B:** strong flow, but it needs booking, the price is unknown, and it's further out.
- **Source:** [castle-climbing.co.uk](https://www.castle-climbing.co.uk/)

## Tier C

**28. Sky Garden.** Free, but tickets are released on Mondays, 3 weeks ahead, with a few weekday walk-ins.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 10:00–18:00, weekends 11:00–21:00.
- **Why C:** it overlaps with 2 and 14, and the booking makes it hard to plan.
- **Source:** [skygarden.london: booking](https://skygarden.london/booking/)

**29. Barbican Conservatory.** A tropical greenhouse inside brutalist concrete.
- **Hours:** only on listed dates (typically Fri 18:30–21:30, Sat–Sun 12:00–19:00), with free timed tickets released a month ahead.
- **Source:** [barbican.org.uk](https://www.barbican.org.uk/whats-on/2026/event/visit-the-conservatory)

**30. Primrose Hill view.** About 63 m, with a protected view to St Paul's.
- **Hours:** open 06:00–22:00.
- **Why C:** it overlaps with 13. Better drafted as a variant of it.
- **Source:** [Royal Parks: Regent's Park and Primrose Hill](https://www.royalparks.org.uk/visit/parks/regents-park-primrose-hill)

**31. Walthamstow Wetlands.** Ten working reservoirs that double as a big nature reserve, with winter wildfowl and peregrines.
- **Hours:** Oct–Mar 09:30–16:00. Free.
- **Arrive:** Tottenham Hale or Blackhorse Road, about a 10-minute walk.
- **Why C:** it's far from the clusters and closes early.
- **Source:** [London Wildlife Trust](https://www.wildlondon.org.uk/nature-reserves/walthamstow-wetlands)

**32. Shoreditch street art wander** *(area vibe)*. Side streets off Brick Lane.
- **Why C:** it overlaps with 16 and the Brick Lane swipe place. Paid tours exist (£15; Wikivoyage), but the moment is a free wander.
- **Source:** [Wikivoyage: East End](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/London/East_End)

**33. Bikes round Hyde Park or Regent's Park.** Santander Cycles: £1.65 per 30 minutes, or a £3 day pass for unlimited 30-minute rides.
- **Why C:** keep it to park paths for strangers' safety. It's also less of a "moment".
- **Source:** [TfL: Santander Cycles pricing](https://santandercycles.tfl.gov.uk/plans)

**34. Postman's Park: the heroes' tiles.** A small garden with a wall of tiles remembering ordinary people who died saving others.
- **Hours:** 08:00 to 19:00 or dusk (Wikivoyage; check). Free.
- **Why C:** moving, but it takes only 10 minutes.
- **Source:** [Wikivoyage: City](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/London/City)
