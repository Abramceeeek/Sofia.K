## Alderman Stairs: where and how

- **Where:** a narrow passage off St Katharine's Way in Wapping, just east of the Tower. It runs between Millers Wharf (78 St Katharine's Way, E1W 1UE) and Tower View. It's about 0.6 miles from Tower Hill station (ianVisits, via search).
- **Access:** the stairs lead down to the river through an unlocked gate, onto the **tidal foreshore**. Beside them, a signposted Thames Path turn leads between the buildings to a **raised terrace**, with a view back up the river to Tower Bridge.
- **The card keeps you on the terrace**, not on the foreshore, which floods with the tide.
- **Coordinates:** the E1W 1UE postcode centroid (postcodes.io). OpenStreetMap was blocked from here.
